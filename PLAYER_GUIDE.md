# 📖 NEXUS - COMPLETE PLAYER'S GUIDE

---

## 🎮 GETTING STARTED

### What is NEXUS?

NEXUS is a narrative philosophical game where your choices shape reality. You'll meet 4 characters (Ivo, Será, Thomas, Reva), each struggling with a philosophical problem:

- **Ivo** — How do you live with guilt you can't forgive?
- **Será** — How do you exist when nobody sees you?
- **Thomas** — How do you think when thinking imprisons you?
- **Reva** — How do you wield power without becoming evil?

Each character's story is 8-30 minutes. You choose how to interact. No choices are "right" or "wrong" — they just have consequences.

**Total playtime:** 2h 45min (your choices determine pacing)

---

## 📚 PHILOSOPHY MENU

The **FILOSOFIA DESVENDADA** button (top-right) opens an educational menu with:

- **8 Philosophers:** Sartre, Camus, Levinas, Buber, Kierkegaard, Heidegger, Nietzsche, Dostoiévski
- **4 Myths:** Sísifo, Prometeu, Orfeu, Pandora
- **Concepts:** Explained as you unlock them
- **Your Journey:** Tracks which philosophers you've discovered

**You don't need to read philosophy to play.** But NEXUS is *about* philosophy, so the more you engage, the deeper you'll understand.

## 🎬 HOW TO PLAY

### Basic Flow:

1. **Watch scene** — Character speaks. Context builds.
2. **Encounter choice** — 3 options appear. Choose one.
3. **See consequence** — Story branches based on your choice.
4. **Unlock philosophical concepts** — As you progress, philosophers become "desbloqueado" (unlocked).
5. **Collect ecos** — Fragmentary memories unlock. Check your ECOS gallery.

### The Philosophy Popups

While playing, you'll see blue text like **[CLIQUE EM "SARTRE"]**. Clicking these opens educational popups explaining the philosopher or concept in context. You can click them or skip — either way, the story continues.

## 🎯 UNDERSTANDING CHOICES

Each character presents 3 choices. They're usually:

- **Option A** — Follow their existing path (reinforces their character)
- **Option B** — Compassionate intervention (tries to help)
- **Option C** — Philosophical challenge (questions their assumptions)

**Important:** No choice is "better." Each path changes the story and the character's final arc.

Example from Será:
- **A:** Accept her isolation, support her sacrifice
- **B:** Reject isolation, demand she live for herself
- **C:** Question whether her compassion is genuine or escapism

Each choice opens different scenes and endings.

## 📊 CHARACTERS & THEIR PATHS

### IVO - "The Weight of Silence" (12 min)

**Problem:** 30 years of guilt crushing him  
**Filósofos:** Sartre, Camus  
**Ecos:** 4  
**Endings:** 3

Choose how to engage with his culpa (guilt). Your choice determines if he accepts Sísifo's eternal return or continues to crumble.

---

### SERÁ - "The Healer's Isolation" (30 min - 3 scenes)

**Problem:** Invisible despite helping billions  
**Filósofos:** Levinas, Buber, Kierkegaard  
**Ecos:** 9  
**Endings:** 3 per scene = 27 possible routes

Será is the longest arc. In Cena 1, you're recognized. In Cena 2, her wound is revealed. In Cena 3, you choose her fate. Can you see her when she can't see herself?

---

### THOMAS - "The Imprisoned Mind" (26 min - 3 scenes)

**Problem:** Knowledge imprisoned him. Reason is his cage.  
**Filósofos:** Heidegger, Wittgenstein, Nietzsche, Descartes  
**Ecos:** 9  
**Endings:** 3 per scene = 27 possible routes

Thomas is intellectually dense. He'll throw philosophy at you. The choice isn't which philosophy is "right" — it's which kind of liberation he deserves.

---

### REVA - "The Last Rebel" (29 min - 3 scenes)

**Problem:** Power is her drug. But power is also her prison.  
**Filósofos:** Nietzsche, Dostoiévski, Foucault, Kafka  
**Ecos:** 11  
**Endings:** 3 per scene = 27 possible routes

Reva is *seductive.* She'll seduce you toward power. Recognize this. Your choices here determine if she rules forever or finally breaks free.

## 🎮 ACT 5: THREE PATHS

After meeting the 4 characters, you choose a path:

### **HARMONY** — Collective salvation
- **H1:** All 4 sacrifice together → NEXUS lives, they become guardians
- **H2:** Only one sacrifices → Hollow victory
- **H3:** Constant vigilance → Imperfect peace maintained through work

### **LIBERATION** — Freedom at all costs
- **L1:** NEXUS collapses, 4 are free but alone in ruins
- **L2:** 4 escape, others are "transferred" (ambiguous)
- **L3:** New system built, radical freedom but fragile

### **PEACE** — Acceptance of cycles
- **P1:** Eternal return — everything repeats forever (Sísifo forever)
- **P2:** Memorial — NEXUS becomes monument to dead
- **P3:** Fourth door opened — mysterious fifth figure changes everything

Each path has 3 endings. That's 9 possible conclusions.

## 🏆 ACHIEVEMENTS & ECOS

### Ecos = Memory Fragments

As you play, you'll unlock **38 ecos** — small narrative fragments. These are:

- Philosophical insights
- Character revelations
- Alternate interpretations
- Hidden memories

Collect them all for **"The Perfect Witness"** achievement.

### Achievements

You have **47 total achievements**:
- **38 Eco-based** (one per memory)
- **6 Path-based** (one per ending type)
- **10 Philosophical** (unlock philosophers/myths)
- **4 Completionist** (100% everything)
- **7 Hidden** (secret conditions)

Check progress in your GALLERY.

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Will my choices affect the ending?**  
A: Yes. Each choice branches the narrative. By Act 5, your accumulated choices determine which paths are available.

**Q: Do I need to understand philosophy to enjoy this?**  
A: No. The story works on emotional level. Philosophy enriches it.

**Q: Is there a "best" ending?**  
A: No. Each of the 9 Act 5 endings is valid. Some are hopeful, some tragic, some ambiguous. Choose based on what resonates.

**Q: Can I reach all 9 endings in one playthrough?**  
A: No. You choose ONE path (Harmony/Liberation/Peace) and ONE ending per path. New Game+ lets you explore others.

**Q: What do ecos do?**  
A: They unlock achievements and deepen understanding. They're optional but rewarding.

**Q: Is the philosophy explained?**  
A: Yes, via popup definitions and character dialogue. You don't need prior knowledge.

**Q: How long is it?**  
A: 2-3 hours per playthrough, depending on reading speed and how many popups you explore.

**Q: What happens if I skip dialogue?**  
A: The story still progresses, but you'll miss context. Recommended first play: don't skip.

## 🌟 TIPS FOR BEST EXPERIENCE

1. **First playthrough:** Don't skip. Let scenes breathe.
2. **Take your time:** This isn't action. It's reflection.
3. **Explore philosophy:** Click the blue text. You'll understand more.
4. **Pay attention to Ivo:** He's short (12 min) but sets up everything.
5. **Notice patterns:** Each character wrestles with isolation. In different forms.
6. **Choose based on YOUR values:** Not "what the game wants."
7. **Play again:** Different choices = different people. Both are "you."
8. **Read the epilogue:** It's 5 minutes of pure philosophy about what you just did.
