# 🎨 NEXUS POLISH & REFINEMENT GUIDE

---

## 📊 DIALOGUE POLISH CHECKLIST

### Standards for all dialogue:

✅ **Authenticity over clarity**
- Characters speak their truth, not exposition
- No "As you know..." exposition dumps
- If unclear, that's intentional

✅ **Rhythm & silence**
- Long pauses matter
- Dialogue isn't filling silence — silence IS dialogue
- Short sentences for tension, long for meditation

✅ **Voice consistency**
- Ivo: Introspective, guilty, trying to explain culpa
- Será: Vulnerable but articulate, searching for recognition
- Thomas: Cerebral, clinical, then breaking
- Reva: Seductive, confident, mask slipping

✅ **Avoid:**
- Clichés ("It's been a long journey...")
- Modern slang (NEXUS is timeless)
- Explaining emotions ("I feel sad...")
- Over-descriptive stage directions

## 🎵 AUDIO POLISH

### Music timing:

- **Cena begins:** Music starts quiet, establishing mood
- **Tension rises:** Gradual crescendo (subtle, not obvious)
- **Climactic choice:** Music swells
- **After choice:** Music shifts to reflect consequence
- **Next scene:** Different piece or variation

### Sound effects:

- **Popup open:** Small chime (not jarring)
- **Achievement unlock:** Satisfying ping
- **Scene transition:** Fade, not cut
- **Silence moments:** Absolute silence (no ambient noise)

---

## 🎨 VISUAL POLISH

### Text styling:

- **Main dialogue:** Clean, readable font (sans-serif)
- **Character names:** Color-coded (Ivo: blue, Será: cyan, Thomas: purple, Reva: red)
- **Philosophy popups:** Different styling (bordered, indented)
- **Ecos unlocked:** Glow effect, brief notification

### Color scheme consistency:

- **IVO scenes:** Cool blues, grays (guilt, coldness)
- **SERÁ scenes:** Cyan/white (light, hope, invisibility)
- **THOMAS scenes:** Purple/silver (intellectual, fractured)
- **REVA scenes:** Reds/blacks (power, seduction, danger)

### Transitions:

- **Fade in/out** for reflective moments
- **Cut** for sudden reveals
- **Scroll** for philosophical popups (elegant)
- **Burst** for choice moments (tension)

## 🎮 GAME DESIGN POLISH

### UI Polish:

✅ **Buttons are clear**
- Choices appear AFTER character finishes speaking
- No rushing the dialogue

✅ **Loading is seamless**
- No visible load screens
- Transition between scenes feels natural

✅ **Feedback is immediate**
- Click choice → instant acknowledgment (button highlights)
- Then brief pause before consequence shown

✅ **Settings accessible but unobtrusive**
- Menu button top-right, not intrusive
- Settings panel slides in, doesn't pop

### Difficulty/Accessibility:

✅ **No difficulty settings (narrative game)**
- All paths are equally "valid"
- No "easier" or "harder" versions

✅ **Accessibility options:**
- Text size: Small/Normal/Large
- Font: Sans-serif/Serif options
- Color-blind mode: Deuteranopia/Protanopia/Tritanopia
- Reading speed: 1.0x / 1.5x / 2.0x (for repeat plays)
- Subtitles: On/Off, background darkening

### Re-play friendliness:

✅ **Skip options on repeat**
- "Skip scene" button appears after first viewing
- Doesn't affect story (you still get ecos)

✅ **New Game+ tracking**
- Shows which paths you've seen
- Suggests unexplored branches

✅ **Achievement tracking visible**
- Progress bar: "6/47 achievements"
- Next easiest achievement shown
