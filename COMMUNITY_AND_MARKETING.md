## 🎨 MARKETING MESSAGING

### Core Message:

> "NEXUS is not a game you win. It's a game you survive. And survive changed."

### Key Selling Points:

1. **Philosophical Depth**
   - Not preachy
   - Philosophers woven into narrative
   - 8 philosophies integrated seamlessly

2. **Real Choices**
   - 12 decision points
   - 9 different endings
   - No "true" path
   - Multiple meanings

3. **Character-Driven**
   - 4 distinct arcs (12-30 min each)
   - Vulnerable, real characters
   - You shape their destiny

4. **Replayability**
   - New Game+ system
   - 47 achievements
   - 38 ecos to discover
   - Different path each time

### Target Audience:

| Demographic | Interest | Why NEXUS |
|-------------|----------|-----------|
| Philosophy students | Ideas | Direct engagement with texts |
| Indie game lovers | Narrative | 2h 45min of dense storytelling |
| Literary fiction readers | Character depth | 4 protagonists with arcs |
| Spiritual seekers | Meaning | No easy answers, just questions |
| Game designers | Innovation | Branching without "best path" |
