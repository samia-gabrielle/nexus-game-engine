## 🌟 FINAL NOTE

NEXUS is not just a game.

It's an experiment in whether philosophy can be interactive without being preachy.

Whether art can ask questions better than answer them.

Whether humans will engage deeply with themes of death, freedom, power, and isolation—if given the choice.

The roadmap above is possibility, not promise.

The real success is: Did you become someone different after playing?

If yes, NEXUS succeeded. Everything else is just elaboration.
